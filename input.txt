We're considering creating an informational booklets about the risks
of CO2 in dutch homes. Below are the considerations we already have in
scope.

---
CO2

- short term vs long term. 5000+ vs 1000ppm
- ndir sensor vs voc derivation; sensor placement in room; use sensor history to track evolution through a night
- legal limits in other countries
- open windows: CO2 vs humidity


Symptoms of medium term exposure (multiple hours, like during the night)
- reduced intelligence
- brain fog
- headaches
- less sleep quality, insomnia
Indirect:
- anxiety
- depression
- anger / sour relationships in the house


Relevance
- not a new problem: do you remember stuffed classrooms in winter when you were young?
- until recently not as much a problem in homes because of poor insulation. More a problem now we insulate better
- always a problem in rooms without windows or closed windows without ventilation gap


Ineffective:
- plants
- open windows once (morning/evening)
- AC
- air filter


Unrelated problems/solutions
- VOCs: mostly in kitchens, can be removed with carbon activated filters. We care more about living spaces (living room, bedroom) and these filters don't apply to CO2
- CO: very poisonous, but also quite rare and the effects are immediately visible. CO detectors don't detect elevated CO2

myth busting:
- "CO₂ is harmless—it’s what we breathe out!" -> Truth: While not toxic like CO, chronic exposure at >1,000 ppm impairs cognition and health
- "I don’t feel anything, so it’s fine." -> Truth: Symptoms are subtle (e.g., brain fog feels like "normal tiredness"). Many people adapt without realizing they’re impaired.
- "My house is old and drafty, so I’m safe." -> Truth: Drafts ≠ ventilation. Old homes can have pockets of high CO₂ (e.g., a sealed bedroom with closed doors).
- "Plants help a little." -> Truth: A 10m² room would need ~100 large plants to match the CO₂ removal of one open window. Effectively zero impact.
- "Air purifiers with CO₂ sensors are enough." -> Truth: Most consumer air purifiers do NOT remove CO₂ (they lack gas-phase filters for CO₂). Only dedicated CO₂ scrubbers (expensive, industrial) work.
